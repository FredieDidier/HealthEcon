Highlights (Journal of Health Economics submission; max 85 characters each)

- Brazil's for-profit maternity sector delivers about 81% of births by cesarean
- Per delivery a cesarean pays less on average; per physician hour it pays more
- Cesareans drop on weekends and holidays, driven by prelabor scheduled surgery
- Within the same municipality-day, the for-profit weekend dip is 1.8 points larger
- Practice style, not case-mix, accounts for 73% of the for-profit-public gap
