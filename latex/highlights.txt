Highlights (Journal of Health Economics submission; max 85 characters each)

- Brazil's for-profit maternity sector delivers about 80% of births by cesarean
- Billed fee gaps are sign-unstable and far too small to explain the rate
- Cesareans drop on weekends and holidays, driven by prelabor scheduled surgery
- Within the same municipality-day, the for-profit weekend dip is 2.3 points larger
- Practice style, not case-mix, accounts for 72% of the for-profit-public gap
