Highlights (Journal of Health Economics submission; max 85 characters each)

- Cesareans account for 81% of births at for-profit hospitals in Brazil
- Cesareans pay physicians less per delivery on average, but may pay more per hour
- Cesarean rates fall on weekends in both public and for-profit hospitals
- Within municipality-days, the for-profit weekend decline is 1.8 pp larger
- Within-Robson cesarean rates account for 73% of the for-profit–public gap
